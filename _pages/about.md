Hello! I'm Zhaofeng Luo, a first-year PhD student at the School of Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Minchen Li.
## Academic Interests
I am currently focused on **robust and efficient computing methods for physics-based simulation**. I also aim to provide **more diverse and realistic simulation methods and scenarios for robotics**.
