Liying Wang, Qing Li, Yuhan Zhou, **Zhaofeng Luo**, Donghao Zhang, Shangguang Wang, Xuanzhe Liu, Chenren Xu

RHONE emulates space computing networks by combining satellite telemetry and hardware-in-the-loop modeling with container-based execution. Its models capture power, thermal, orbital, network, and computational behavior at both satellite and constellation scales.
