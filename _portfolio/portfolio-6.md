## A Self-Adaptive Retro-FSO Design for Air-to-Ground Communication

**Publication:** ACM MobiCom '23 - Proceedings of the 29th Annual International Conference on Mobile Computing and Networking

**Paper:** [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3570361.3614067)

### Authors
Zhe Ou, Zhaofeng Luo, Guanyu Shi, Chenren Xu

### Overview
This work presents a novel self-adaptive retroreflective free-space optical (Retro-FSO) communication system designed for high-speed air-to-ground connectivity. The system addresses the challenges of maintaining stable optical links between moving aerial platforms and ground stations.

### Key Contributions
- **Self-Adaptive Design**: Automatic alignment and tracking for mobile platforms
- **Retroreflective Technology**: Enhanced link reliability and reduced complexity
- **Air-to-Ground Focus**: Optimized for aerial vehicle communication scenarios
- **High-Speed Communication**: Enables fast data transfer in challenging environments
